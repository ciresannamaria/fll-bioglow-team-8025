Game missions

Use this page as a mission overview. Check the official mission document and rulebook for the exact setup, conditions and scoring.

1. Mighty Microbiomes

Place keystone species in the microbiomes. The mission has an additional queen bonus condition.

2. Roots of Renewal

Move resources up the Grand Tree into the Canopy Chamber.

3. Cave Waterfall

Send keystone species through the waterfall and check the final counter.

4. Rainforest Awakening

Release keystone species from the Nest and resources from the Hollow Tree.

5. Central Haven

Work together: tokens resting fully in Central Haven score for both teams.
