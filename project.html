Project

The Engineering Notebook introduces four project pathways. These are project topics, not the four match roles. The team explores the pathways and chooses a project direction to research.

🚁 Driver pathway

How could you use drones or other aerial technology to help the rainforest?

🌳 Operator pathway

How could you start a conservation project to help a nearby ecosystem?

🔎 Technician pathway

How could you monitor an ecosystem to improve the lives of plants and animals?

🌱 Specialist pathway

How could you restore a piece of land to benefit the native plants and animals?
