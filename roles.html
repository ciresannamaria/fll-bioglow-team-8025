Team roles

The four game roles are Driver, Operator, Technician and Specialist. The role cards provide guided starting points; they do not limit which missions a player may attempt.

🚗 Driver

Drives the driving base using the controller.

🛠️ Operator

Uses a non-electric LEGO tool to move game items.

⚙️ Technician

Works with the single motor and colour sensor.

💻 Specialist

Works with the Grand Tree motor, controller and team device/programs.

Assignment checklist

  • Choose two students for each role.
  • Practise explaining what each role does.
  • Practise switching and supporting one another.

Student names and assignments should be kept in a private team record, not on this public page.
